My Favorite Poem

"I'm the Jack of All Trades"

— Ryan Martin



"Im the jack of all trades,
Master of none.
A light for others,
Yet seen by none.
You mend, you give, you stand so tall,
But who will catch you if you fall?
I pour my heart, yet run so dry,
A silent echo, a lonesome cry."
